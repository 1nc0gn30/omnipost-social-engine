## 🧠 The Psychology of Social Feed Attention

Algorithmic feeds (Twitter/X, LinkedIn, BlueSky, Threads) reward posts with high **Dwell Time** and early **Expand/Click Rates**. When a user stops scrolling to read or clicks "Show more", platform recommendation engines register positive affinity and broadcast the content to second-degree connections.

To consistently trigger this behavior, hooks leverage 4 cognitive biases:

```
┌───────────────────────────┬─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Cognitive Mechanism       │ Psychological Trigger                                  │
├───────────────────────────┼─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Information Gap Theory    │ Piques curiosity by highlighting unknown knowledge.     │
│ Loss Aversion Bias        │ Highlights costly mistakes or obsolete practices.       │
│ Authority & Social Proof  │ Leverages empirical hours spent, scale, or metrics.     │
│ Cognitive Friction Relief │ Promises a structured, bite-sized simplification.       │
└───────────────────────────┴─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## 📏 Character Budgeting & Typography Best Practices

1. **The 70-Character Rule:** On mobile feeds, only the first **70–120 characters** are visible before truncation ("Show more" or card border). Ensure your primary curiosity hook fits within this boundary.
2. **Whitespace Pacing:** Break sentences into single or double lines. Avoid dense 5-line paragraphs.
3. **Emoji Restraint:** Use 1–2 purposeful emojis (e.g. 🧵, 🚀, 👇, 📊) as visual anchors; never use emojis as word replacements.
4. **Call to Action (CTA):** Always end with a clear instruction:
   - *"Bookmark for later 🔖"*
   - *"Repost (1/N) to share with your network 🔁"*
   - *"Drop your thoughts below 👇"*
